The faithfulness trap

Faithfulness asks “is every claim supported?” A refusal makes no claims, so it scores 1.0 automatically. BM25 posts the best faithfulness score by refusing 38% of the questions it could have answered. Read the two bars together, never apart.

Faithfulness ↑ betterWrongly refused ↓ better

Never report faithfulness without a false-refusal rate beside it. This is a property of the metric, not of any one implementation — it applies to RAGAS and DeepEval faithfulness scores identically.

Chunk size — both measures peak together

Standard wisdom says small chunks give tight citations but miss answers, and large chunks do the reverse. Both peak at 700 characters. Citation accuracy doubles from 400 to 700, then falls — it is highest when a chunk is about as long as an answer.

Where each retriever wins

Recall@5 split by question type. Dense beats BM25 on lexical questions — the category keyword search is supposed to own. Auto-captions mangle the exact terms BM25 needs: tiktoken → “Tik token”, BPE → “B PA en coding”.

Why a similarity threshold can’t separate them

Every question’s top retrieval confidence, dense retriever. If a cutoff could tell answerable from unanswerable, the two rows would not overlap. They overlap between 0.55 and 0.70.

The embedding model never scores below ~0.45 on ordinary English, even for unrelated text. A threshold of 0.28 — the original guess — sat below the entire distribution and never fired once.
